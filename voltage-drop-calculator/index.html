Worked example

A 5m LED-strip run at 12V pulling 5A over 18 AWG (0.75 mm²): round-trip 10m × 0.023 Ω/m = 0.23Ω → 5A × 0.23Ω = 1.16V drop = 9.6%. The verdict: undersized — step up to 14 AWG (2.5 mm²) for 2.9% or feed the strip from both ends.

FAQ

What is the 3% voltage drop rule?

The standard low-voltage wiring convention: 3% on the branch, 5% total. More than that and gear misbehaves — motors heat, LEDs dim, sensors drift.

One-way or round-trip length?

Round trip — current goes out and comes back. Enter the physical run length; this tool doubles it internally.

Why do connectors matter?

A cheap barrel connector can add 10-30 mΩ — at 10A that's 0.3V, more than meters of good wire. Crimp or solder high-current joints.
