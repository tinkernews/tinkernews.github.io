⚠ The three rules of LED-strip power. 1. Never power a long strip from one end — the flex copper drops volts; the far LEDs shift color or flicker. Inject along the run.
2. Common ground between the supply, the strip and the microcontroller. A level shifter on the data line (3.3→5V) fixes most glitching.
3. Fuse the supply line near the PSU. Strips fail short; an unfused 5V 20A supply is a fire risk.

Worked example

5m of 60 LED/m = 300 LEDs at 5V. Realistic animations (20mA): 6.0A = 30W. Worst-case full white (60mA): 18A = 90W. With 30% headroom: pick a 5V 12A (60W) supply for animations-only, or 5V 24A if full white is ever on the menu. Inject at 0m, 2.5m, 5m.

FAQ

How many amps does a 5m WS2812B strip draw?

300 LEDs: 6A realistic (animations) / 18A worst case (full white) at 5V.

How often should I inject power?

Every 2–3 meters at 5V for 60 LED/m, always at both ends. Voltage drop is the limit — not the supply.

What fuse should I use?

~20% above worst-case draw, within 15cm of the PSU. 300 LEDs full white = 20–25A fuse (or fused sub-branches).
