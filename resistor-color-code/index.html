FAQ

What do 4 red-brown-black-gold bands mean?

Red=2, brown=1, black=0 → 21 × 10⁰ = 21 Ω, gold = ±5% → 19.95–22.05 Ω. Nearest E12 value: 22 Ω.

Which side is the tolerance band?

The lone band at one end — usually gold or silver, slightly separated. Read from the end closest to the banded group.

How do I read a 5-band resistor?

Three digit bands (d1 d2 d3) then multiplier, then tolerance: 1-0-0 ×10¹ = 1000 Ω. 5-band values are typically 1%-tolerance metal film.
