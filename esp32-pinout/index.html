Jump to safe pins Strapping warnings
⚠ Three rules before you wire anything.
1. Never use GPIO 6–11 — they connect the ESP32 to its flash chip. Using them crashes the board.
2. Strapping pins (0, 2, 5, 12, 15) are read at reset: they set boot mode and flash voltage. External pulls on these can stop the board booting.
3. GPIO 12 pulled high at boot = 3.3V flash modules may not start. Leave it floating unless you know your flash voltage.
✅ Safe general-purpose pins: GPIO 4, 16, 17, 18, 19, 21, 22, 23, 25, 26, 27, 32, 33. Need analog with WiFi on? Use ADC1 (GPIO 32–39) — ADC2 conflicts with WiFi.

FAQ

Which pins are safe at boot?

GPIO 0, 2, 5, 12, 15 are strapping pins read during reset. GPIO 6–11 belong to flash. The safe everyday set: 4, 16, 17, 18, 19, 21, 22, 23, 25, 26, 27, 32, 33.

Why does ADC2 stop working with WiFi?

The WiFi radio shares the ADC2 SAR. Use ADC1 (GPIO 32–39) for analog reads on connected projects.

Can GPIO 34–39 use internal pull-ups?

No — the input-only pins lack internal pull resistors. Add external ones (10kΩ typical).
