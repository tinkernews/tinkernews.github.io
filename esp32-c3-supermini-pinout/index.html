⚠ The three rules for the C3 SuperMini.
1. GPIO 2, 8, 9 are strapping pins (GPIO 9 = BOOT button). External pulls on these can change boot mode or block flashing.
2. GPIO 11–21 are the flash/PSRAM bus on modules — never wire them; the board won't boot or will corrupt.
3. 3.3V logic only. The C3 is not 5V-tolerant — level-shift 5V sensors before connecting.
✅ Safe everyday pins: GPIO 0, 1, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 10. ADC1 is on GPIO 0–4 (no ADC2 on the C3 = no WiFi conflict — analog reads just work).

FAQ

Which pins are safe at boot?

GPIO 0, 1, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 10 are unconditionally safe. GPIO 2/8/9 work for outputs but keep their boot-time levels in mind; GPIO 11+ are off-limits.

Why does my C3 not flash with something wired?

Nine times out of ten it's GPIO 9 (BOOT) pulled by your circuit — disconnect it during flashing — or GPIO 2/8 strapped wrong.

Can I read analog while WiFi is on?

Yes — the C3's single ADC has no WiFi conflict (unlike the classic ESP32's ADC2). Use GPIO 0–4.
